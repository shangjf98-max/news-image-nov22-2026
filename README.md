Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[news-image-20261122](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122):
sum up Sunday 22 November 2026's world news in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) draws a 1024×1024
newspaper front page — 46 stories in three layers (3 leads, 11 picture
cards, 32 briefs), a UTC timeline, a numbers strip, a dotted world map,
a date-check box and a confidence legend — and writes it to
`/output/entry.png`. It is deterministic: two runs produce identical bytes.

Run it with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## The day, in brief

- COP31 in Antalya collapses with no deal — no fossil-fuel line, no pact.
- Iran says two more tankers hit mines near Hormuz; Bahrain seeks UN action.
- A Russian drone flattens an apartment block in Dymer, north of Kyiv.
- Poland confirms the three objects that fell on its soil were Russian
  Kh-101 cruise missiles; Tarik Skubal agrees a record $450M Mets deal.

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on 22 November
2026; claims are attributed and older news is listed in the DATE CHECK box
on the page itself. Sources: Reuters, AP, CNN, Le Monde, WSJ, NBC Sports,
Al Jazeera, AFP, UKMTO, Tasnim, Lowy Institute.
